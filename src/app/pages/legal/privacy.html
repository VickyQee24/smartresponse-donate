Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: {{ updated }}

1. Introduction

{{ site.organisation.longName }} ("the Foundation", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

2. Information we collect

  • Donation details — your name, email address, the amount and the frequency of your gift.
  • Order details — your name, email address, phone number and delivery address when you buy a product.
  • Volunteer applications — your name, contact details, location, availability, areas of interest and anything you tell us in the message field.
  • Contact messages — your name, email address, subject and message.
  • Technical data — basic information your browser sends, such as device type and pages viewed.

We do not collect or store your card details. Card payments are handled entirely by Paystack on their own systems.

3. How we use your information

  • To process donations and purchases, and to issue receipts.
  • To manage recurring donations you have set up.
  • To arrange delivery of products you have ordered.
  • To respond to your enquiries and volunteer applications.
  • To send you updates about our work, where you have asked to hear from us.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and regulatory reporting obligations.

4. Legal basis

We process your data to perform a contract with you (for example fulfilling an order), to meet legal obligations (such as financial record keeping), on the basis of your consent (such as marketing emails), and for our legitimate interest in running and reporting on the Foundation's charitable work.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Paystack, our payment processor, to take payments securely.
  • Delivery partners, where needed to deliver an order to you.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and regulators, where we are required to do so.

6. Beneficiary and safeguarding data

We treat information about the girls, young people and communities we serve with particular care. Photographs, stories and testimonials are only published with appropriate consent, and we anonymise details where publishing them could put anyone at risk.

7. How long we keep it

We keep donation and purchase records for as long as required for financial and legal reporting. Volunteer and enquiry records are kept only for as long as they remain relevant, after which they are deleted.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your data where we have no need to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent, including unsubscribing from our emails.
  • Cancel a recurring donation at any time.
  • Object to how we are processing your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email {{ site.contact.email }}. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and local storage

This website uses only what is necessary for the site to function. Paystack may set its own cookies during checkout in order to process payments securely and prevent fraud. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

10. External links

Our website links to other services, including our WhatsApp channel and our podcast on Spotify. Those services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure, but we work to reduce risk and to respond promptly if a problem arises.

12. Children

Our programmes serve young people, but this website is not intended for unsupervised use by children under 13. Where our work involves minors, we obtain consent from a parent, guardian or the responsible institution.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version published on this page is the version that applies.
