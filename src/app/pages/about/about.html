Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide fast, innovative, and accessible emergency reporting and response solutions that protect lives, property, and communities in Nigeria and beyond.

Vision

Bridging the critical gap in National safety response infrastructure in order to become the world's leading emergency response technology platform, connecting people, smart wearables, ambulances, paramedics, and emergency services to deliver rapid, coordinated assistance whenever and wherever it is needed.

OUR STORY

Young people creating meaningful change.

Smart Response Youth Foundation is a youth-centred organization focused on community development, humanitarian support, empowerment and social impact. We work for the upholding of the dignity of marginalized youth and the girl child.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, opportunity, safety and a chance to contribute to society. Our work brings young people, communities and partners together to identify challenges and develop practical solutions.

The foundation started in 2025 and is affiliated to the 112 Smart Response App, an emergency reporting platform built to connect people who need help with the services that can reach them.
