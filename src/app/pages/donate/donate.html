SAVE HER DIGNITY CAMPAIGN

Save her dignity. Empower her future.

Every girl deserves to learn, dream and thrive without the burden of menstrual poverty.

Through the Save Her Dignity Campaign, Smart Response Youth Foundation is empowering Nigerian youth — especially adolescent girls and vulnerable young people — with menstrual health support, health access, digital education and civic empowerment.

Your support can help provide sanitary products, health assistance through free or supported medication, the Save the Dignity campaign launch, digital education and civic empowerment for vulnerable Nigerian youth via the forum section of the app.

Together, we can help girls stay in school, protect dignity, and create opportunities for a healthier, more equal future. Join us in giving every girl the dignity to stay in school and every young person the opportunity to become a positive force for change.

Save a dignity. Empower a future. Donate today. Be the change.
